# UNDERTOW — Story Bible: Voice & Style Guide

*The lake is horrifying. The paperwork is worse.*

---

## 1. Core Philosophy: Municipal Horror & Gallows Humour

UNDERTOW is a tragedy played as an administrative dispute. 

The horror does not come from howling monsters, tentacles, or eldritch incantations; it comes from **cold, procedural inertia meeting catastrophic unnatural grief**. The drowned town of Greywater Hollow is not trapped in an inferno; it is catalogued, ledgered, and administered by an entity that is scrupulously, resentfully fair.

The comedy and the horror are the same organ. When the Office of Returns sends a stamped notice itemizing forty-seven drowned souls by net weight and memory retention, it is simultaneously absurd bureaucratic satire and an excruciating reminder that you opened the spillway thirty years ago.

---

## 2. The Register of The Office of Returns

The entity at the bottom of the lake (known colloquially as **The Mouth**) communicates exclusively through water-stained bottle notes, receipts, and stamped forms bearing the masthead of **The Office of Returns**.

### Voice Characteristics
- **Municipal & Colonial Bureaucracy:** Reads like a 19th-century municipal water authority, provincial clerk, or colonial customs office.
- **Aggressively Neutral:** Uses passive voice and formal distancing (`"The Office observes..."`, `"It has been brought to our attention..."`, `"Notice is hereby served..."`).
- **Escalating Politeness:** The Office never yells. As its resentment and alarm grow, it becomes *more* formal, *more* scrupulous, and *more* punctiliously polite.
- **Relentlessly Transactional:** Everything has an inventory value. Catches are assessed by `weight`, `memory content`, and `struggle`. Rebuilding a house is an asset restoration; returning a soul is a completed return order.
- **No Malice, No Mercy:** The Office is not an enemy looking to destroy the keeper. It is an overburdened clerk compelled by ancient law to fulfill a contract it hates.

### Formatting & Visual Conventions
- **Header Blocks:** `[FORM #]`, `[NOTICE OF ELIGIBILITY]`, `[SCHEDULE D: ITEMIZATION]`, `[RE: REF-####]`.
- **Form Field Language:** Quantities in words and numerals (`one (1) brass thimble`, `four hundred (400) souls`).
- **Stamps & Annotations:** Stamped marginalia in bold or uppercase (`[RESTRICTED. NICE TRY.]`, `[AUDITED]`, `[DELIVERY PENDING]`).

## 3. Bestiary Entry Rules

Bestiary entries are the primary narrative engine of UNDERTOW (~60 species across 5 zones).

### The Golden Rules
1. **1 to 3 Sentences Maximum:** Brevity creates impact. Never overwrite.
2. **The Singularity Rule (Funny OR Quietly Awful):** 
   - An entry may be deadpan comic, or it may be quietly devastating.
   - **It must NEVER be both winking at once.** Never undercut a scary observation with a punchline, and never ruin a dry joke with melodramatic brooding.
3. **Clinical Observation:** Frame entries like the notes of an exhausted naturalist or a town registrar cataloguing fauna. Describe anatomical wrongness as if it were a minor municipal zoning infraction.
4. **The Depth Trajectory (The Lerp of Wrongness):**
   - **Shallows (Zone 1):** Dry, observational, gallows comedy, local folklore (*"Uncle Aldous swore he'd catch it or die trying. Both, as it turns out."*).
   - **Kelp Graves (Zone 2):** Unsettling, crowded, collective memory, faint ecclesiastical dread.
   - **Township (Zone 3):** The pivot. Entries stop being jokes. Fish wear town habits. Recognizable human traces appear (*"Pale Carp — its scales spell a name you refuse to read."*).
   - **The Choir (Zone 4):** Lyrical, terrifying, mournful, unreadable.
   - **The Mouth (Zone 5):** Crimson, terminal, final accounts.

### Clean Catch vs. Willing Variants
- **Fought Entries:** The default entry unlocked upon hooking and landing/butchering the catch.
- **Willing Entries (Maren's Thimble):** A rare variant unlocked when a catch surrenders without fighting. Willing entries are **always worse**—they reveal that the fish recognized you, remembered the flood, and came to the surface out of pity or obedience.

## 4. Banned Moves (The "Never" List)

To preserve the delicate balance of tone, the following are strictly prohibited:

- ❌ **NO Fourth-Wall Breaks:** The game does not know it is a video game. No references to "players", "HP", "controllers", "respawns", or "levels". Meta-progression is framed diegetically as *License Renewals* and *Condolence Rates*.
- ❌ **NO Modern Slang or Internet Irony:** No "cringe", "oof", "wholesome", "gamer", "vibes", or modern tech/corporate buzzwords ("synergy", "paradigm"). The vocabulary is strictly Edwardian/Victorian provincial waterworks.
- ❌ **NO Explaining the Joke:** Never add commentary like *"which was rather strange"*, *"if you can believe it"*, or exclamation points. Let the absurd fact stand alone.
- ❌ **NO Melodramatic / Gothic Purple Prose:** Avoid overwrought adjectives (*"unspeakable cosmic horror"*, *"bloodcurdling eldritch nightmare"*). Call it *"one (1) specimen, damp, possessing sixteen teeth not native to the genus."*
- ❌ **NO Pure Villainy:** The fish are not evil. The Mouth is not an evil overlord. The drowned are not zombies. They are people who drowned thirty years ago and have found a quiet, terrible communion at the bottom.

## 5. Naming Conventions for Greywater Hollow

Greywater Hollow was a secluded northern English / Anglo-provincial valley town in the early 20th century.

### Personal Names
- **Traditional, Sturdy, Scriptural, or Maritime:**
  - *Men:* Aldous, Enoch, Silas, Clement, Arthur, Walter, Jesse, Thomas, Ambrose, Gideon, Miller, Bram, Frank.
  - *Women:* Maren, Martha, Hettie, Clara, Edith, Elsie, Agnes, Beatrice, Cora, Winifred, Abigail, Mabel.
- **No Modern or Fantasy Names:** No "Kai", "Jax", "Aiden", "Zephyr", or high-fantasy elven constructions.

### Surnames & Toponyms
- **Geographic & Occupational:** Mercer, Finch, Blackwood, Coldwater, Vance, Callow, Silt, Weaver, Thorne, Fletcher, Oakes, Carver.
- **Places:** Greywater Hollow, The High Spillway, Willow Street, Low Pier, The North Sluice, Mill Race, The Lyceum, Saint Jude-in-the-Fens.

### Official Titles
- *The Keeper of the Light* (The Player)
- *The Office of Returns* (The Warden/Mouth)
- *The Clerk of Sluices and Weirs*
- *The District Postmaster*
- *The Silt Surveyor*
- *The Bell-Ringer of Saint Jude*
