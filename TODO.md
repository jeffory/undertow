# Visual QA Review — Undertow

## Visual QA — 2026-08-26 (Post-Camera & Shoreline Pass)

### Critical (breaks the fantasy)
- **Luma Balance & Contrast Range (Crushed Blacks & Zero Bright Highlights):** Objective luma gate (`tools/beauty.mjs`) reports 83.6% of pixels below 0.15 luma (concept ref ~60%) and 0.0% above 0.85 luma (concept ref ~2%) (`tools/beauty-frame.png`, `tools/beauty-vs-concept.png`). The scene is uniformly underexposed and crushed into flat black with zero specular punch, whereas the concept art (`docs/concepts/images/night_boat_combat_1787661324075.jpg`, `tether_combat_gameplay_1787661298454.jpg`) carries its dramatic value contrast in glowing lantern warmth and sharp wave-crest foam highlights.
  *Suggested fix:* Provide the bright ~2% spike (>0.85 luma) by (1) adding an unshaded high-luma emissive core mesh (`#fff2a8`, luma > 0.95) to the boat/keeper lantern with warm specular highlights on wet boat gunwales and oilskins, and (2) adding thresholded pure white (`#ffffff`, 1.0 luma) specular glints and foam caps on open-water Gerstner wave crests in `src/render/water.ts`. Lift ambient/fog floor slightly from `#000000` to deep indigo/teal (`#040a10` to `#081218`) so mid-ground wave facets read in silhouette.
- **Procedural Fish Species & Morphology (Ghost-White Tubes):** Caught fish render as featureless, smooth ghost-white crescent streaks or flat grey tubes with 2D triangle tails (`tools/t2c-foot.png`, `tools/sweep2.png`, `tools/fish-bestiary.png`), lacking jaws, teeth, glowing eyes, distinct head profiles, or 3D fin geometry vs the menacing grotesque silhouettes in the bestiary (`docs/concepts/images/bagman_creature_bestiary_1787661398932.jpg`, `crawler_creature_concept_1787661429903.jpg`, `night_boat_combat_1787661324075.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Upgrade `FishParams` procedural generator to construct distinct head profiles (blunt snout, hammerhead, needle jaw), sharp polygonal teeth/jaws, glowing emissive polygonal eyes (contributing to high-luma accents), and attached 3D fin meshes (dorsal, pectoral, caudal) with dark mottled vertex palettes and species-specific wrongness lerp.
- **Procedural Line Render & Rig Attachment:** Tether line (`tools/fish-fight-v2.png`, `tools/t2c-foot.png`, `tools/gate-a-landing.png`) renders as a thick, blurry translucent green tube emitting directly from the keeper's torso / boat center with no rod or winch rig in hand, destroying the taut laser-filament combat feel (`docs/concepts/images/tether_combat_gameplay_1787661298454.jpg`, `night_boat_combat_1787661324075.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Narrow procedural Bézier to a crisp 1–2px glowing filament with bright neon core / bloom (lerping green `#33ff88` to red `#ff3344` under snap tension), and anchor origin strictly to the tip of a held fishing rod (foot) or winch spool (boat).

### High (visibly below the concept bar)
- **Keeper In-Scene Readability & Stance:** In-engine keeper (`tools/t2c-foot.png`, `assets/review/keeper.png`) remains frozen in a rigid T/A-pose with a pitch-black void face and bell helmet instead of the wide floppy sou'wester, held fishing rod, and dynamic combat bracing stances depicted in concept art (`docs/concepts/images/tether_combat_gameplay_1787661298454.jpg`, `night_boat_combat_1787661324075.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Rig keeper with distinct active idle stances (manning the winch on boat, bracing with arched rod on foot), remodel hat brim to floppy sou'wester silhouette, and attach handheld lantern prop emitting warm point light.
- **Shoreline Foam Artifacts & Islet Terrain Geometry:** While the T2 shoreline depth mask prevents water from overflowing islets, the resulting surf foam renders as harsh, disjointed white polygon triangles and stepped clipping artifacts at the water-rock boundary (`tools/t2c-foot.png`, `tools/beauty-frame.png`, `tools/t2e-boat-moving.png`), while the islet plateau remains a flat untextured brown polygon.
  *Suggested fix:* Smooth shoreline depth transition with dithered or soft-edged noise band in `src/render/water.ts` to eliminate jagged white triangles, and replace the flat islet top with stepped, faceted slate rock geometry (`#1c2226` to `#2d353b`) consistent with the rock clusters around the lighthouse base (`docs/concepts/images/lighthouse_hub_dusk_1787661369817.jpg`).
- **Sky Dome Edge & Horizon Transition:** Lowering the camera to ~26° exposes the sky and horizon, but reveals a harsh geometric black dome perimeter cutting across the flat teal background void (`tools/beauty-frame.png`, `tools/t2e-boat-moving.png`) rather than the rich dusk/indigo sky gradient and distant mountain silhouettes in concept art (`docs/concepts/images/lighthouse_hub_dusk_1787661369817.jpg`, `night_boat_combat_1787661324075.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Replace hard sky dome cutoff with a smooth vertical gradient shader (deep charcoal `#06090c` through indigo `#121a24` to misty teal `#182830`), blend distance fog seamlessly into the horizon, and add low-poly distant mountain ridge silhouettes.
- **Diegetic HUD & In-Game UI Overlays:** Game viewport in debug mode is dominated by monospace sliders and debug panels (`tools/t2e-boat-moving.png`, `tools/t2c-foot.png`), while standard gameplay lacks the minimal diegetic inventory chips (lantern, bait, hook/reel) and grungy health/tension gauges established in concept art (`docs/concepts/images/tether_combat_gameplay_1787661298454.jpg`, `night_boat_combat_1787661324075.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Keep developer tuning panels strictly gated behind `?debug`, and implement styled corner-chip DOM HUD for inventory/status items with grungy paper borders matching the Bureaucratic/Office aesthetic.

### Medium (polish)
- **Boat Waterline Contact, Buoyancy & Winch Rig:** Rowboat in beauty shot lacks mechanical winch spool on stern, stowed oars, and waterline contact foam ring (`tools/beauty-frame.png`, `tools/beauty-vs-concept.png`), sitting rigidly in water without buoyancy roll or oar locks.
  *Suggested fix:* Mount mechanical winch drum and mast rigging to boat deck, mount paired oars along gunwales, and add subtle pitch/roll rocking response to Gerstner wave displacement with a contact foam rim.
- **Combat Splash & Fish Breach FX:** Hooking, lunging, and landing fish creates no water splash bursts, foam shards, or expanding disturbance rings (`tools/fish-fight-v2.png`, `tools/gate-a-landing.png`), leaving combat disconnected from the lake surface (`docs/concepts/images/tether_combat_gameplay_1787661298454.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Spawn faceted low-poly particle splash arcs and expanding concentric foam ring decals on fish breach, lunge, and landing events.
- **Office Paperwork Styling & Typography:** Tribute receipts and death condolence letters (`tools/run-receipt.png`, `tools/run-condolence.png`, `tools/m4-grade-up.png`) have clean vector layouts but appear overly pristine vs the water-stained, stamped documents concept (`docs/concepts/images/the_mouth_office_concept_1787661509027.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Add subtle CSS grunge effects—slight paper rotation/jitter, coffee/water stains, rough stamped ink edges on `[AUDITED]` stamps, and weathered typewriter typography.

### Low (nice-to-have)
- **Environmental Props & Navigation Buoys:** Open lake expanse (`tools/lake-boat.png`, `tools/t2e-boat-moving.png`) is barren of secondary nautical props depicted in concept art (`docs/concepts/images/lighthouse_hub_dusk_1787661369817.jpg`, `crawler_creature_concept_1787661429903.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Place low-poly bell buoys with glowing caged lanterns, floating timber, and rusted channel markers to delineate zones and provide secondary light sources.
- **Bestiary 3D Pedestal & Lighting:** Register of Species modal (`tools/fish-bestiary.png`) displays catches as static dark specimens on generic round bases without dramatic staging (`docs/concepts/images/bagman_creature_bestiary_1787661398932.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Add dedicated rim-lit 3D preview viewport with slow turntable rotation and mossy stone pedestal mesh.
