# Visual QA Review — Undertow

## Visual QA — 2026-08-26

### Critical (breaks the fantasy)
- **Water Surface Shader & Palette:** Shipped lake (`tools/lake-boat.png`, `tools/lake-foot.png`) renders as a flat, pale grey-blue gradient plane with zero wave facets or foam crests, failing the near-black/dark-teal low-poly ocean with sharp white foam highlights established in the art bible (`docs/concepts/images/tether_combat_gameplay_1787661298454.jpg`, `night_boat_combat_1787661324075.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Replace the smooth gradient plane with a flat-shaded low-poly vertex-displaced mesh using near-black `#081014` / deep teal `#0e1f26` base, directional rim specular, and thresholded white foam facets on disturbance crests.
- **Islet Terrain & Ground Readability:** Hub islet (`tools/lake-foot.png`, `tools/line-v4.png`, `tools/foot-integrated.png`) is a giant, completely flat untextured brown octagon (`#3c2e1e`) resembling a raw collision box rather than the craggy, faceted slate-rock promontory shown in the hub concept (`docs/concepts/images/lighthouse_hub_dusk_1787661369817.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Replace the 2D polygon with stepped, faceted low-poly rock geometry in dark slate tones (`#1c2226` to `#2d353b`), steep shoreline cliffs, and instanced rock clusters (`assets/review/rocks.png`) breaking up the waterline.
- **Procedural Line Render & Attachment:** Tension line (`tools/line-v4.png`, `tools/fish-fight-v2.png`, `tools/gate-a-landing.png`) renders as a fat, blurry translucent green tube emitting directly from the keeper's torso with no rod or winch in hand, destroying the taut laser-filament combat feel (`docs/concepts/images/tether_combat_gameplay_1787661298454.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Narrow the procedural Bézier to a crisp 1–2px glowing filament with bright neon core / bloom, and anchor origin strictly to the tip of a held fishing rod (foot) or winch spool (boat).
- **Procedural Fish Species & Morphology:** Current catches (`tools/sweep2.png`, `tools/species-sweep.png`, `tools/fish-bestiary.png`) are featureless, smoothed grey tubes with flat 2D triangle tails, lacking eyes, dorsal/pectoral fins, teeth/jaws, or distinct silhouettes across species vs the grotesque bestiary bar (`docs/concepts/images/bagman_creature_bestiary_1787661398932.jpg`, `crawler_creature_concept_1787661429903.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Upgrade `FishParams` procedural generator to construct distinct head profiles (blunt/snout/beak), sharp teeth/jaws, glowing polygonal eyes, and 3D fin attachments (dorsal/pectoral/caudal) with high-contrast vertex palettes and wrongness lerp.

### High (visibly below the concept bar)
- **Keeper In-Scene Readability & Pose:** In-engine keeper (`tools/foot-integrated.png`, `tools/line-v4.png`, `assets/review/keeper.png`) is tiny, stuck in a T/A-pose with a pitch-black void face and bell-shaped pith helmet instead of the wide floppy sou'wester, lit from beneath by a ground bulb rather than a handheld lantern (`docs/concepts/images/tether_combat_gameplay_1787661298454.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Scale keeper up ~20%, reshape hat brim to classic sou'wester silhouette, attach warm point light to a held lantern prop with cast shadow, and rig basic combat/reeling idle stance (ref `docs/plan/07-assets.md` §Milestone 1.5).
- **Lighthouse Scale, Placement & Mesh Artifacts:** Lighthouse (`tools/lake-foot.png`, `tools/line-v4.png`, `assets/review/lighthouse.png`) sits ungrounded in the center of the mud flat with floating cyan artifact polygons at its base, lacking the monumental height and coastal cliff placement of the dusk concept (`docs/concepts/images/lighthouse_hub_dusk_1787661369817.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Scale up lighthouse 1.5x–2.0x, anchor it atop an elevated rocky crag at the island's edge, remove duplicate/floating cyan base polygons, and align its jetty dock with the shoreline.
- **Night Sky, Fog Atmosphere & Volumetrics:** World background (`tools/lake-boat.png`, `tools/lake-foot.png`) is an empty, flat dark grey void with no horizon transition, no dusk/indigo sky gradient, and no visible sweeping lighthouse volumetric beam (`docs/concepts/images/lighthouse_hub_dusk_1787661369817.jpg`, `night_boat_combat_1787661324075.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Retune ExponentialSquared fog with dark-teal atmosphere, add a subtle dusk/night sky keyframe gradient (ref `docs/plan/08-polish.md` §6), and add a soft volumetric light cone for the sweeping beacon.
- **HUD & In-Game UI Overlays:** Game viewport (`tools/lake-boat.png`, `tools/line-v4.png`) is dominated by raw monospace terminal sliders and debug text, lacking the minimal diegetic corner chips (hook, lantern, bait) and grungy health/tension bars seen in concept UI (`docs/concepts/images/tether_combat_gameplay_1787661298454.jpg`, `docs/plan/08-polish.md` §5).
  *Suggested fix:* Hide developer tuning panels behind a debug hotkey (`?debug` only), and implement styled corner-chip DOM HUD for inventory/status items with grungy paper borders matching the Office aesthetic.

### Medium (polish)
- **Rowboat Waterline Integration & Oar Rig:** Rowboat (`tools/boat-no-oar.png`, `tools/line-v4.png`, `assets/review/rowboat.png`) sits rigidly on top of the water plane with a single disconnected oar sticking out at a right angle or missing oars entirely, lacking contact buoyancy roll or waterline displacement (`docs/concepts/images/night_boat_combat_1787661324075.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Remove the rogue single oar, mount paired oars or winch rig along the gunwale, and add subtle pitch/roll rocking with a small waterline contact foam mesh.
- **Splash, Foam & Combat FX:** Hooking, lunging, and landing fish (`tools/fish-fight-v2.png`, `tools/gate-a-landing.png`) creates no water disturbance, foam shards, or splash particles, leaving tether combat feeling disconnected from the lake surface (`docs/concepts/images/tether_combat_gameplay_1787661298454.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Trigger faceted low-poly splash particle bursts and expanding concentric white foam ring decals on fish breach, lunge, and landing events (ref `docs/plan/08-polish.md` §6).
- **Office Paperwork Styling & Typography:** Tribute receipts and condolence letters (`tools/run-receipt.png`, `tools/run-condolence.png`, `tools/m4-grade-up.png`) have solid municipal layout but appear overly clean/digital with flat grid paper and pristine borders vs the water-stained, stamped documents concept (`docs/concepts/images/the_mouth_office_concept_1787661509027.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Add subtle CSS grunge effects—slight paper rotation/jitter, coffee/water stains, rough stamped ink edges on `[AUDITED]` stamps, and weathered typewriter font styling (ref `docs/plan/08-polish.md` §1).

### Low (nice-to-have)
- **Bestiary 3D Pedestal & Lighting:** Register of Species modal (`tools/m4-bestiary.png`, `tools/fish-bestiary.png`) displays catches as static dark silhouettes or flat grey specimens on generic round bases without dramatic staging (`docs/concepts/images/bagman_creature_bestiary_1787661398932.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Add dedicated rim-lit 3D preview viewport with slow specimen turntable rotation and mossy stone pedestal mesh for examined catches.
- **Environmental Props & Buoys:** Lake expanse (`tools/lake-boat.png`) is barren of secondary nautical props (floating bottle buoys, bell buoys, sunken wooden pilings, reed beds) depicted in concept art (`docs/concepts/images/lighthouse_hub_dusk_1787661369817.jpg`, `crawler_creature_concept_1787661429903.jpg`).
  *Suggested fix:* Add low-poly bell buoy with clapper lantern and scattered floating timber/reeds to break up open water and signal zone boundaries.
